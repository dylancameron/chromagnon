# Chromagnon privacy policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

Chromagnon doesn't collect, sell or share any data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it makes no network requests.

## What it stores, and where

Chromagnon keeps a few things so it works the way you left it. They're stored only on your device, in Chrome's local extension storage:

- Your settings: copy format, keyboard shortcut, and whether the shortcut is on.
- Your last 24 picked colors.
- Palettes you create.
- The two colors in the Contrast tab.
- Your theme and the last tab you had open in the side panel.

This data never leaves your device and isn't synced to your Google account. You can clear your recent colors and delete palettes at any time in the extension. Removing Chromagnon deletes all of it.

## Access to web pages

Chromagnon runs a small script on web pages so the pick shortcut works there. That script only checks whether a key press matches your shortcut. It doesn't read page content, and it doesn't record or send your keystrokes. You can turn the shortcut off in Settings.

The eyedropper is Chrome's own, and it only reads the color of the pixel you click.

## Clipboard

When you pick a color, Chromagnon copies it to your clipboard. It never reads your clipboard.

## Links

The Settings tab links to Buy Me a Coffee. Nothing is sent there unless you open the link, and that site's own privacy policy applies once you do.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions go to [GitHub issues](https://github.com/dylancameron/chromagnon/issues).
